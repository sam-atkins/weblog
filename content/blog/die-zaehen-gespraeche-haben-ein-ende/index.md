> Die zähen Gespräche haben ein Ende

The tough discussions have an end.

Yann Sommer is joining Bayern for EUR 8 million plus bonus payments. The Swiss keeper will sign a contract until 2025.

Thank goodness. Really couldn’t see Bayern winning anything this season without signing a top class keeper.

Will certainly make things interesting when Neuer gets back to full fitness. Of course, that should read _if_ as there is no guarantee he can return to his previous exceptionally high standard.
